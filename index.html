    Practice before the ACJM Court and Judicial Magistrate First Class benches in Rampurhat includes Hon’ble JM I, JM II and JM III. The stated criminal practice areas include police G.R. matters, criminal complaint proceedings, remand, charge hearings and trials under the Indian Penal Code (IPC) and the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS).

    IPC sections: 498A, 379, 411, 279, 304A, 323, 324, 325, 341, 354, 427, 448, 506 and 34.
